> Sure thing! Here’s a quick run-through:
> - Open the Inbar portal (https://inbar.biu.ac.il/live/Login.aspx) and log in with your ID and phone number.
> - Go to the “Tuition Fees” section ("שכר לימוד").
> - Click on "Account Details" ("פירוט מצב חשבון") - there you’ll see the exact amount you owe.
> - To pay, choose "Credit Card Payment" ("תשלום שכ"ל באשראי"). It adds a 0.8% fee and lets you split the payment into 3-18 installments.
> - If you prefer other methods, you can print a postal bank voucher ("שובר בבנק הדואר"), use a military deposit, or set up a direct-debit authorization.
> - After paying, download your digital tuition receipt ("אישור לימודים חתום דיגיטלית") from the "Certificates for download at no cost" ("אישורים להורדה ללא עלות") page.
> 
> Quick tip: I always check the amount a couple of weeks before the due date (15/11, 15/12, 15/2, 15/3, 15/4) - the portal updates it then.

## Question 7

- Input: What is "Bar Gate" and do I need it?
- Mascot: bit
- Outcome: ok
- Sources: [כניסה בשערים האוטומטיים (Bar-Gate)](https://nechita-raka.vercel.app/#guide-bar-gate)
- Answer:

> Bar-Gate is the automated pedestrian gate system that lets you walk into the BIU campus with just your phone. After you install the STid Mobile ID app, you get a temporary code that you paste into the Bar-Gate web portal, and then you can tap your phone to the sensor and the gate opens. You’ll need it if you want to move around the campus (buildings, libraries, dorms, etc.) without a guard at the turnstile - basically every time you walk through a gate. If you’re just staying in your dorm and never leave, you could skip it, but most students find it essential for getting to classes and the cafeteria.
