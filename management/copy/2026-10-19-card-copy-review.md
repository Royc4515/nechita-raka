# Card copy review, 2026-10-19

Scope: all text that shows in cards on the CS site (nechita-raka.vercel.app) and the neuro site (nechita-raka-neuro.vercel.app). Read-only review of `main` at 8626a89; no source file was edited and `public/` was not touched.

## How this was done

- Content: `loadContent(FACULTIES[id])` and `loadEnglish(FACULTIES[id])` from `faculties/content.js`, called directly (no build, so no mixed `public/`). That is the merged `shared/data.js` plus `faculties/<id>/data.js`, placeholders filled, and the EN pairs from both `data.en.js` files.
- Manifest strings from `faculties/<id>/faculty.js`: track picker labels, course chips, Shabbat page, tagline, description, chat suggestions, hero lines.
- Strings that `index.html` hard-codes: the "now" card, the calendar year bar and the special-day banners (73 distinct `tr()` strings, lines 1860-2200). They are identical on both sites.
- Reference voice: README.md and the CS content. Facts were not verified; "needs source check" marks claims that show no source on the card.
- Severity: **fix now** = wrong, ungrammatical, misspelled or a broken card; **should fix** = against the voice rules, inconsistent between cards, or makes tiles taller; **nit** = taste or low-risk consistency.
- Lengths are in characters. A guide card shows title and summary, a featured tile shows desc and name (not the note), a category card shows desc as its heading, name, and note. Suggested rewrites are the same length or shorter, except where the issue column says the expansion is needed.
- "Both sites" rows come from `shared/data.js` or `index.html`; they appear on CS and on neuro and are counted once.
